OUR BRAND PORTFOLIO

A Growing Portfolio.

From dining to fast casual, we bring world-class brands to the UAE and build our own from here. Each one is run by a team that knows how to turn great food into moments people come back for.

PAN-ASIAN COMFORT FOOD WITH A KICK!

Wagamama

Wagamama is one of the world’s most recognized Pan-Asian restaurant brands, known for bold, fresh flavors, iconic dishes and a distinctive dining experience built around good food and good energy. As Wagamama’s largest franchisee, RMAL operates 12 restaurants across the UAE, with a footprint that stretches from neighborhood communities to some of the country’s most prestigious malls and destinations.

Over the years, we have continued to grow Wagamama’s presence and relevance in the UAE, creating experiences that connect with local customers across dine-in, digital and delivery while staying true to the distinctive Wagamama spirit.

The OG of nightlife. Over two decades of an unmatched vibe.

Trader Vic's

For more than two decades, Trader Vic’s has been a regional favourite and a defining name in Dubai’s nightlife scene — famous for an energy and atmosphere that simply can’t be replicated.

Rooted in Polynesian-inspired dining and cocktail culture, the experience brings together bold island flavours, legendary cocktails, the iconic Tiki bar, signature Trader Vic’s rituals and the unmistakable sound of its live Cuban band.

From the first Mai Tai to the Cuban rhythms that carry the night, generations have made Trader Vic’s part of their own rituals.

More than a restaurant. A nightlife original with a vibe all its own.

A LEBANESE RESTO-CAFÉ FOR EVERY MOOD

Mazaj

Rooted in the green courtyards and open-hearted warmth of Lebanon, Mazaj takes its name from the Arabic word for mood: a place where time slows down and every mood finds its place.

One of RMAL’s homegrown brands, Mazaj is a relaxed Lebanese resto-café where family lunches linger into dinner over mezze and warm manakeesh, and evenings drift by in clouds of shisha from a wide variety of flavours, the click of backgammon dice and conversations that never quite end. It is a place to gather, to unwind after a long day, and to simply be in the mood.

Craveable Korean BBQ in a CUP

Cupbop

Rooted in the flavors and energy of Korean street food and born in the United States, Cupbop reimagined Korean BBQ as a bold, accessible fast-casual experience.

Since RMAL brought the brand to the UAE, Cupbop has quickly built recognition for its customizable flavors, playful personality and highly interactive experience. Its growing presence at concerts, festivals and major events has brought the brand to new audiences and helped establish Cupbop as a distinctive name in the UAE’s fast-casual scene.

A defining figure in modern gastronomy

Marco Pierre White

Marco Pierre White changed the face of British dining and became the youngest chef of his time to be awarded three Michelin stars.

RMAL’s partnership with Marco Pierre White brings together his culinary legacy and internationally recognised name with RMAL’s concept development, commercial and operating expertise.

Together, the partnership provides a platform to create distinctive hospitality concepts built around the Marco Pierre White brand, with the potential to develop and grow across markets.
