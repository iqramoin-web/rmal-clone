Cookies Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. About this policy

This Cookies Policy explains how RMAL Hospitality PJSC ("RMAL", "we", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on this website (the "Website"). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle your personal information.

2. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit it. They are widely used to make websites work, to remember your preferences and to give site owners information about how the site is used. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local and session storage, work in much the same way, and we refer to all of them as "cookies" in this policy.

3. How we use cookies

We group the cookies we use into the following categories.

Necessary

These are needed for the Website to work and cannot be switched off in our systems. They include remembering your language, keeping the Website secure and remembering, for the length of your visit, that you have already seen the welcome animation so that it does not repeat on every page.

Third-party content

Some pages may show content from other providers, such as embedded maps or videos. Those providers may set their own cookies, which we do not control. Please read their policies for details.

Functional

These remember choices you make, such as your preferred language, so that the Website works the way you expect when you come back.

Analytics

Analytics cookies are only set if you accept them, analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use the Website, for example which pages are visited most and where visitors encounter problems. The information is combined and used only to improve the Website. We will only set these cookies if you have agreed to them where the law requires your consent.

Marketing

With your consent, marketing cookies may be set by us or by our advertising partners. They are used to show you relevant ads on other websites and social media, and to measure how well our campaigns perform. They may track your visits across websites. We will only set these cookies if you switch them on in the cookie banner.


4. How long cookies last

  • Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies stay on your device for a set period, which can range from a few days to up to 12 months, or until you delete them.

5. Managing your cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you see which cookies are stored, delete some or all of them, and block cookies from particular websites or from all websites. How you do this depends on your browser, so please check its help pages.

If you block or delete cookies, some features of the Website may not work properly, and your preferences, such as your language, may not be remembered.

When you first visit, our cookie banner lets you accept all cookies, keep necessary cookies only, or customize your choices. You can change your preferences at any time via the cookie settings link.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time, for example when we start using a new type of cookie. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

7. Contact us

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please write to RMAL Hospitality PJSC, Office # 608, Bayan Building, DIP 1, Dubai, United Arab Emirates, or email info@rmalhospitality.ae.
