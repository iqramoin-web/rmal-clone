ABOUT

RMAL Hospitality is a UAE-based, family-owned F&B group built through hands-on experience in one of the world's most dynamic hospitality markets.

Over the years, we have grown our portfolio, expanded our capabilities and built the infrastructure required to manage increasingly diverse F&B businesses. Today, RMAL combines the long-term perspective of family ownership with the governance, commercial discipline and professional capabilities of a growing corporate group.

OUR STORY

From F&B Operator to Integrated Hospitality Group

Our story has been shaped by our passion for growing businesses while creating memorable experiences for our guests.

Years of managing restaurants and international brands have given RMAL experience across every part of the F&B journey, operations, culinary, finance, people, procurement, marketing, customer strategy, commercial development and growth.

As the business evolved, so did our capabilities. What began with operating brands has developed into a broader platform capable of building concepts, growing brands, advising businesses and managing F&B investments.

OUR ROOTS

Rooted in the UAE. Defined by Our Ambition.

The UAE is home to customers, brands and talent from around the world. Building a hospitality business here requires an understanding of different cultures, tastes, occasions and expectations. RMAL's knowledge comes from operating within that environment every day.

It gives us a perspective that combines local understanding with international brand experience - helping us identify opportunities, adapt intelligently and build businesses relevant to the markets and customers they serve.

OUR VALUES

Values That Shape Our Legacy.

At RMAL Hospitality, our values are more than guiding principles. They are the foundation of our identity, the strength behind our relationships and the driving force behind our ambitions. Deeply embedded in our culture, they shape every decision we make, every experience we create and every partnership we build. They define not only how we do business, but the lasting impact we aspire to leave.

  • Responsible Profit

    Maximizing profit while maintaining integrity.

  • Making guest-first decisions

    Delighting the guest before focusing on tasks.

  • Ample opportunities

    Providing opportunities for people to reach their potential.

  • Long-term focus

    Making decisions that ensure we are in business for a long time.

OUR AMBITION

Building Brands and Businesses Beyond Borders

Our next chapter is about extending the capabilities we have built into new opportunities. That means strengthening our existing portfolio, developing new concepts, forming new partnerships, entering new markets and building businesses with the potential to scale internationally.

Growth matters to us. Building businesses with enduring value matters more.
