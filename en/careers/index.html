NATIONAL TALENT - RUWAD ADDIYAFAH

Developing Emirati

Ruwad Addiyafah is a 12-month, full-time paid employment programme designed for Emirati nationals at the beginning of their careers who want to understand how F&B businesses are created, operated and grown.

APPLY EXCLUSIVELY VIA NAFIS

FROM REALITY TO IDEA

Participants gain hands-on experience across key areas of the business, developing a broad understanding of F&B before progressing into a specialised pathway supported by practical training and professional mentorship.

  1. 01

    Apply & Start

    Variety of cuisines, brands and locations across Dubai & Abu Dhabi

    12 Month Employment Program

    Work, learn, and understand the fundamentals of succeeding in the F&B industry.

  3. 03

    Pathways & options

    After committing for 12 months, they can pick their future path.

Three Pathways.

  • Build with RMAL

    Develop your own F&B concept with RMAL's support and access to the expertise behind our businesses.

  • Build Independently

    Take the knowledge, experience and capabilities gained through the programme and use them to pursue your own entrepreneurial journey.

  • Continue with RMAL

    Build your career within RMAL and continue developing within our growing portfolio and business.
